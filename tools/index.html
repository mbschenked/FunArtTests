Game feel tools

Interactive tools for tuning how a game feels, by Max Schenk. Each one runs in your browser with nothing to install. A computer with a keyboard works best.

For Unreal Engine

Work out values in the browser, then copy them into the editor.

  • Camera Shake Lab

    A copy of the Details panel for an Unreal Engine 5 camera shake, with the engine's defaults. Change any value and the camera view and timeline redraw.

    Try: pick the Gun kick preset, then Hit from the left, and read the gotchas under the views.

  • Trajectory Sandbox

    Shape the flight path of a Death From Above takedown, the leap from a ledge onto an enemy below. Play it back at any distance and height, then export it as an Unreal CurveVector .csv. Use a computer: on a phone the graph doesn't fit.

    Try: drag a point on the curve, press Space to play the jump, and move the slider left of the graph to change the drop.

Uncharted 4 VFX study

Real-time rebuilds of two effects from Uncharted 4, matched against captured gameplay frames.

  • Muzzle flash and tracer range

    Uncharted 4's rifle muzzle flash and bullet tracer, rebuilt in real time and fired together on a test range with six cameras and dusk, night and day lighting. Every part of each effect is a slider, with presets.

    Try: press A to auto-fire, E to slow time to 0.05×, keys 1 to 6 to switch cameras, and N to change the lighting.

The source for every tool is in the FunArtTests repo on GitHub.
