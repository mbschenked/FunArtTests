Assumed, confirm or change

  • Height blend: Low and High reference drops are 2 m and 8 m, weight linear in drop and clamped. All four references start as copies, so nothing changes until a High one is edited.
  • Duration: each reference has its own T (Near 0.4 s, Far 1.2 s; High starts equal to Low). A Span's T mixes them by distance and drop.
  • Span presets: 2 m (drop 2 m), 5 m ledge (drop 3.5 m = 5 m − the 1.5 m target offset), 15 m (drop 5 m), 30 m (drop 8 m).
  • Re-time rule is chord-length, measured in world cm at the Span being edited.
  • New point defaults to "re-smooth curve". "Keep shape" locks the new point's tangent and its neighbours'; dragging a locked point unlocks it.
  • Monotone applies to both forward and Lift, so the arc can't rise above its highest Control point.
  • Velocity arrows show where the attacker would be 0.1 s later at that velocity.
  • The ledge edge is at Launch; the capsule is 180 cm tall, 34 cm radius, centred on P(t).
  • The four references share their Control point times: a time edit anywhere moves all four by the same amount. That keeps the one-Equation form exact.
  • A shape edit in Mixed is split across the four references by their weights (least change): it all lands on one reference at its own distance and drop. T in the Span bar splits the same way.
  • Blend pairs Control points by index; a new point lands at the same fraction of the segment in both Trajectories.
  • "All Spans" saves four stretch-ready Curve Vectors (Near/Far × Low/High) on the same keys. UE samples all four at s = t/T, mixes them by (1−w)(1−h), (1−w)h, w(1−h), wh, then stretches to the Span.
  • Even-distance keys are spaced along the curve at the Span shown; a stretch-ready file used at another Span is only roughly even there. The keys/s rate becomes the average.
  • Export keys per second: 120 to start. Stretch-ready files space their keys for the longer reference duration, so every Span gets at least that rate. "dots/s" only changes the Time dots on screen.
  • The vertical slider left of the graph sets the drop (Launch height above Arrival), from −3 m to 15 m. Up = a higher Launch.
  • Picking a Shape also sets its Span: Neutral arc → 5 m ledge (the start-up Span), each Probe → 10 m forward, 2 m drop (the research probe's Span).
  • Typing a section's duration moves its end point and the next section absorbs the change, so T stays. The last section moves its start point instead.
  • Option-drag slides a point's time without moving it: dragging the width of the Span changes its time by the whole duration. Time edits keep a locked tangent.
  • A Control point can't pass its neighbours in time (1 % gap); Shift locks a drag to one axis (on an arrow, it keeps the direction).
